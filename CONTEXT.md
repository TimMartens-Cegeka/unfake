# AI Photo Assessment

This context defines the language used by the public app that assesses whether a photo was generated or edited with AI.

## Image assessment

**AI-generated image**:
A photo-like image whose visual content was created by an AI system rather than captured as a scene.
_Avoid_: AI-edited photo

**AI-edited photo**:
A captured photo changed after capture using AI, including enhancement and retouching; automatic processing performed by a camera as it captures the photo is not an AI edit in this context.
_Avoid_: manipulated photo, fake photo

**AI signal**:
An indicator that may support an assessment that an image was generated or edited with AI; it is evidence, not proof of how the image was made.
_Avoid_: proof, authenticity check

**Assessment**:
An uncertain conclusion about AI signals in an image, which may report signals detected, no reliable signals found, or insufficient evidence to assess.
_Avoid_: authenticity verdict, certification
